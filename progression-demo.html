⚖️ Harmony Calculation

The Tri-Vector Harmony score prevents one pillar from dominating. It gates rank promotions and encourages balanced progression.

harmony_score = (min_score × 0.4) + (avg_score × 0.6) Example for this user: Vector Levels: Mind 20, Muscle 20, Money 21 Normalized Scores (0-100): 67, 72, 73 Min: 67 | Avg: (67+72+73)/3 = 70.67 Harmony = (67 × 0.4) + (70.67 × 0.6) = 26.8 + 42.4 = 78.2% ≈ 78%
Key Feature: Low harmony doesn't delete XP—it just gates higher ranks. This user has 45K XP but can't reach Elite (rank B) until harmony reaches 75%. By maintaining balanced growth across all three vectors, players progress faster through the ranks.
